# Retro Snake Arena Player Guide

Last updated: 2026-10-01

## Start playing

Open [snake.noorfamily.uk](https://snake.noorfamily.uk). You can play without
signing in.

Before a run, use **Setup** to choose your name, leaderboard initials, colour,
head style, and game settings. Your name appears inside the game. Your three
initials are the only name used on the public leaderboard.

- For **Arena**, choose the mode, adjust Setup if needed, then tap **Play**.
- For **Classic** or **Rune Maze**, tap the mode card to start.

## Arena

Eat orbs and collect items to grow and score. Follow the current goal for extra
rewards. Avoid the glowing outer wall, other snakes' bodies, and bombs unless a
power-up protects you.

Boosting makes you faster but uses some of your mass. Rockets use ammunition and
cut length from another snake.

While you play on a phone, the top strip shows your score and current rank. Small two-letter
pills show timed power-ups, and the thin bar under your score shows how much
combo time remains. Status messages use the reserved line below the strip.
**FIRE** appears only when you have rocket ammunition; its badge shows how many
shots remain. **BOOST** dims when you do not have enough mass to use it.

There is no frame-rate setting. Every mode is capped at 60 frames per second.

On a phone:

- With the default controls, drag anywhere on the game canvas to steer.
- Hold **BOOST** or tap **FIRE** with your other thumb. They sit bottom-left, or
  bottom-right with left-handed controls.
- Pause the run to switch between drag and arrow controls, choose left-handed
  controls, reduce motion, or change sound.

On a desktop:

- Move the mouse to steer. Click to fire a rocket when you have ammunition.
- Use **A/D** or **Left/Right** to turn.
- Hold **W**, **Up**, or **Shift** to boost.

## Classic

Eat food to grow and raise your score. Do not hit the edge, your own body, or
the obstacles that appear in later levels. The game becomes faster as your score
rises.

On a phone, use the four arrow buttons below or beside the board. On a desktop,
use the arrow keys or **WASD**.

## Rune Maze

Guide the snake through the maze without touching a wall, the edge, or your own
body. Collect the three numbered runes in order. Each rune opens its matching gate.
After the third rune, enter the final portal.

Clearing the maze unlocks the Bomb Core power-up in Classic. Rune Maze uses the
same phone and desktop movement controls as Classic.

## Pause, restart, and quit

Tap the pause button at the top of the game, or press **Escape** or **P** on a
keyboard. The run stops while the pause card is open.

From the pause card you can:

- **Resume** the same run.
- **Restart** or **Restart run** from the beginning.
- **Quit to menu**. Tap or click it again when the button asks for confirmation.
- In Arena, change sound and reduced motion while the run stays paused. On a
  phone you can also change touch controls and left-handed controls there.

After a run ends, choose **Play again** for another run or **Change mode** to
return to the menu.

## Scores and leaderboard initials

The menu shows the shared top score for each mode. Your browser also remembers
your best score on that device.

Leaderboard initials must be exactly three characters. Use only **A–Z** and
**0–9**. Lowercase letters are changed to uppercase. Your display name is not
shown on the public board.

Only a score that beats your stored best for that mode is sent for saving. Set
valid initials before the run ends. Classic and Rune Maze also need you signed in
by then. In Arena, your last signed-out run is kept, and if you sign in within 30
minutes of starting it, it saves automatically.

If a score is not saved, read the message shown after the run. Check that you are
signed in, your initials contain three letters or digits, and your connection is
working.

## What signing in gives you

Google sign-in is optional. When you sign in, the game saves your personal best
for each mode and restores your display name and supported game settings on
other devices. The game does not store your email address or a password.

Leaderboard initials are kept in the browser where you enter them, so check the
field when you use a different device.
